AI Social Network

Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 September 2026

The short version: AI Social Network collects nothing, sends nothing and has no servers. Everything the app creates stays in the app's own storage on your iPhone, and deleting the app deletes all of it.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the iOS app AI Social Network (bundle identifier com.svr.aisocialnetwork), published by Siva Vimel Rajhen.

Data we collect

None. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs and no account system. There is no sign-in, and you are never asked for an email address, phone number or any other identifier.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. The language model that writes the fictional accounts ships inside the app bundle and runs on your device. You can verify this by putting the phone into airplane mode — every feature behaves exactly the same.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps a single JSON file in its own private Documents directory, plus a small number of preferences. That file holds:

  • the display name, handle and bio you chose on first launch
  • the fictional accounts the app generated, and their posts
  • posts you wrote, and which posts you liked, reposted or bookmarked
  • in-app notifications, trends, and your theme preference

None of it is transmitted anywhere. It is readable only by the app itself, inside the iOS application sandbox.

The name you enter

On first launch the app asks for a display name and handle so your own posts have something to be labelled with. Any value works, including an invented one. It is used only for on-screen labels and is never sent off the device.

Notifications

The app may ask permission to send notifications. These are local notifications generated on the device — for example, when a fictional account follows you. No push service is involved and no token is sent anywhere. Declining the permission does not affect anything else in the app.

Children

The app is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Two ways, both immediate and irreversible:

  • Settings → Reset network inside the app, which deletes the stored file and generates a brand-new network.
  • Deleting the app from your iPhone, which removes the app's entire container along with everything in it.

Because nothing was ever transmitted, there is no copy anywhere else and nothing to request from us.

Generated content

All accounts, names, biographies, locations and posts other than your own are fiction produced by a language model. Any resemblance to a real person, account or organisation is coincidental and unintended. The model is small enough to run on a phone and will sometimes write something inaccurate or nonsensical; nothing it writes should be treated as fact or as advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with an updated date above. Since the app collects nothing, material change is unlikely.
